# Project Summary

I built an unsupervised machine-learning analysis and deployed the results as a public research paper.

I used aggregated FlyRank content-performance data covering 50,000 content items and 14 features related to search visibility, traffic, acquisition, and user engagement.

The analysis showed two broad content-performance archetypes, with one group demonstrating substantially stronger observed performance, providing a useful basis for further content investigation and prioritization.
